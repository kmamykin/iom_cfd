The balance at one point

    
Each part's loads at a solved point. Along the course and across it they cancel, the roll moment equals the righting moment RMφ and the yaw moment is zero; the Σ bars are the residuals. The vertical force and the pitch moment go to the hydrostatics, which set sinkage and trim. Pick a point here or click one on the polar.

The force models

    
Each part's loads around one of the race's best-VMG states. One input varies at a time and the others stay at the solved state; sinkage and trim follow from the hydrostatics. Off the state the boat is not in balance: these curves are the force models' own response, and the ringed point is the solved state. Hollow points read a part's table outside its range.

Frames and nomenclature

    

      
Forces (X, Y, Z) are in the course frame: X along the course, the direction of Vb, + forward; Y horizontal and across it, + to windward (port, on port tack); Z up. Positions (x, y, z) are in the boat frame: x along the centreline, + aft from the bow; y + to port; z + up from the waterline. Neither frame heels: heel turns the boat's points about the centreline and the axes stay level. Moments K, M, N are about the bow on the waterline. Everything is for port tack; starboard is the mirror image.
